Independent research Reproduction & extension · Privacy in ML

Membership Leakage via Model Explanations

Can a model's explanations reveal whether a record was in its training data?

An empirical reproduction of Shokri, Strobel & Zick (2021), On the Privacy Risks of Model Explanations, extended with an attribution-clipping defence inspired by DeepLeak (Ben Hmida et al., IEEE SaTML 2026).

  • Trained target and shadow CNNs on disjoint CIFAR-10 splits; computed Saliency and Integrated Gradients attributions with Captum.
  • Built membership-inference attacks on explanation summary statistics and compared them with loss, logit-scaled (LiRA-style) and confidence-only baselines across three seeds.
  • Reported accuracy and TPR at 0.1%, 1% and 5% FPR, following current MIA evaluation practice.
  • Identified a softmax-saturation tie artifact that zeroes the loss attack's low-FPR TPR, and fixed it with a logit-scaled score.
  • Evaluated attribution clipping (90th–99th percentile) as a mitigation: it reduced attack accuracy by under one point, suggesting simple clipping is not enough.
  • PyTorch
  • Captum
  • scikit-learn
  • Membership inference
  • Explainable AI
